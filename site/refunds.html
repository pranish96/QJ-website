Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Cancel anytime. Unused paid days are refunded if you ask within 7 days of a payment.

Cancelling

You can cancel from the extension's settings or by emailing us. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and nothing renews after that.

Refunds

  • Within 7 days of a payment: full refund of the unused days in that period.
  • If QuickJobs didn't work for a site you paid for, for reasons on our side, we refund that part, whenever you ask.
  • One-time items such as the cover letter are refundable until they have been written.
  • Credits already used for AI work are not refunded.

How refunds are paid

To the same payment method, within 5–7 business days of approval.

How to ask

Email quickjobsbeta@gmail.com from your account email with the payment date. We reply within 2 business days.
