What QuickJobs does

QuickJobs runs in your own Chrome browser. With your permission it reads job listings on sites you choose, scores them against your profile, and, for jobs you approve, applies on your behalf. If you turn them on, it can also read LinkedIn posts, email or message the people who posted jobs from your own Gmail and LinkedIn, and handle routine recruiter replies.

  • The information in your profile and CV must be true. QuickJobs sends it to employers as you gave it.
