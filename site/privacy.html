Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your email address.
  • Profile and CV: what you enter at setup, such as name, phone, city, experience, skills, salary expectation and CV files.
  • Job activity: jobs found, their scores, what you approved, and where Quickjobs applied.
  • Answers and templates: answers to screening questions and your email and message templates.
  • If you turn them on: LinkedIn posts that match your roles, and the subject and sender of job-related unread emails in your Gmail.

What we don't collect

Passwords for job sites, Gmail or LinkedIn; your browsing history; and card or bank details (payments are handled by our payment provider).

How it is used

Only to run Quickjobs for you: finding and scoring jobs, applying, writing answers and messages, and sending you summaries. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase (database and server), hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Anthropic (AI), which receives job text and the parts of your profile needed to score a job or write an answer.
  • Brevo (email), for sign-in and summary emails.
  • Our payment provider, for payments.

Your choices

Each kind of data needs your consent at setup, and you can withdraw it in settings. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to quickjobsbeta@gmail.com; we reply within 7 days and delete within 30.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Each customer can read only their own records. Access to the server is limited to the people running Quickjobs.
