Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your email address.
  • Profile and CV: what you enter at setup, such as name, phone, city, experience, skills, salary expectation and CV files.
  • Job activity: jobs found, their scores, what you approved, and where QuickJobs applied.
  • Answers and templates: answers to screening questions and your email and message templates.
  • If you turn them on: LinkedIn posts that match your roles, and the subject and sender of job-related unread emails in your Gmail.

What we don't collect

Passwords for job sites, Gmail or LinkedIn; your browsing history; and card or bank details (payments are handled by our payment provider).

How it is used

Only to run QuickJobs for you: finding and scoring jobs, applying, writing answers and messages, and sending you summaries. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase (database and server), hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Anthropic (AI), which receives job text and the parts of your profile needed to score a job or write an answer.
  • Brevo (email), for sign-in and summary emails.
  • Our payment provider, for payments.
  • Google (Analytics and Tag Manager), for visit statistics on our website.

Cookies and website analytics

Our website, quickjobs.pro, uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see which pages are used. It sets cookies and records things like pages viewed, approximate location, device, browser and the site you came from. It runs only on our website pages and never receives your profile, CV or job data. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on.

Waitlist and how people use this website

When you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, the referral code we give you, the code of the friend who invited you (if any) and where you came from. We use them to let you into the beta and to send your beta code, and we email you about nothing else.

Our website also records a few steps of each visit in our own database: opening a page, joining the waitlist and clicking Add to Chrome. With each step we keep the page, the site you came from, any campaign tags in the link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) and whether you are on a phone or a computer. To link the steps of one visit, the site keeps a random ID in your browser’s local storage (named qj_vid). It is not your name or email, and we don’t link these records to your email or to anything you give QuickJobs. We use them only to see where people get stuck, so we can make joining and installing easier. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes the ID.

Your choices

Each kind of data needs your consent at setup, and you can withdraw it in settings. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to quickjobsbeta@gmail.com; we reply within 7 days and delete within 30.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Each customer can read only their own records. Access to the server is limited to the people running QuickJobs.
