How it works

One run a day, finished before your chai.

QuickJobs is a Chrome extension. It works through the job sites you're already signed in to, so it applies as you. Scroll through one real run.

Privacy first

Your job search is sensitive. QuickJobs is built to keep it private.

You're trusting us with your CV, your pay and the fact that you're looking. This is exactly how that data is handled.

No passwords, ever

We never ask for your job-site, Gmail or LinkedIn passwords. QuickJobs works through the sites you're already signed in to in Chrome.

Your CV and pay stay on your computer

Your CV files, phone number, date of birth and current pay are kept in your own Chrome, not on our servers.

You choose what's stored

Each kind of data, like your job history or saved answers, is stored only after you switch it on, and you can switch it off again.

The AI sees only what it needs

To score a job, the AI gets the job description and your roles, title, experience, skills, cities and expected pay. Never your phone number, current pay, date of birth or gender.

Stored in India, never sold

Your data is stored in India, never sold and never used for ads. This site has no ad or tracking scripts.

Delete everything, any time

Delete your account and your data goes with it. If you stop your plan, everything of yours is deleted from our server after 30 days.

For beta testers

Install the beta in about ten minutes.

QuickJobs isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so beta testers add it to Chrome by hand. You need Chrome on a laptop or desktop (Windows, Mac or Linux) and your invite email, which has the download link and your invite code.

AAdd QuickJobs to Chrome

  1. Download and unzip it

    Open the link in your invite email to download the zip. Unzip it into a folder you'll keep, such as Documents › QuickJobs. On Windows, right-click the zip and choose Extract All; on a Mac, double-click it.

    Don't delete or move this folder later. Chrome runs QuickJobs from it.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    In Chrome, type this in the address bar and press Enter, then switch on Developer mode at the top right.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Click Load unpacked

    Three buttons appear. Click Load unpacked and select the QuickJobs folder you unzipped: the folder itself, the one with manifest.json inside, not the zip.

  4. Check that QuickJobs is on

    QuickJobs (beta) now shows on the page with its switch turned on. If Chrome shows an error instead, you picked the wrong folder: go back a step.

  5. Pin it to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to QuickJobs. Its green bolt stays in your toolbar from then on.

BCreate your account

  1. Enter your email

    Click the QuickJobs icon, choose Create account, type your email and click Send me a code. Use the address your invite was sent to.

  2. Type the 6-digit code

    Check your inbox for a 6-digit code, and Spam or Promotions too. Type it in and click Sign in. No password needed.

  3. Add your CV and profile

    Setup opens in a new tab. Upload your CV as a PDF and QuickJobs fills in what it can read. Check the rest: city, experience, pay in lakhs and notice period.

  4. Choose roles, cities and sites

    Under Job preferences, add up to five roles and the cities you'd work in, then pick your job sites. Leave Apply without asking me off until you trust the matches; until then you approve each job.

  5. Enter your invite code

    On the next page, type the invite code from your email and click Start my beta. Everything is free during the beta.

CYour first run

  1. Sign in to your job sites

    In the same Chrome, sign in to the sites you picked, the way you normally do. QuickJobs uses those sign-ins and never asks for your passwords.

    • Naukri
    • iimjobs
    • Shine
    • Foundit

  2. Click Run now

    Click the QuickJobs icon, then Run now. The first run reads every site, scores the jobs and fills your dashboard. Keep Chrome open while it runs; you can watch each step as it happens.

    After this, it runs by itself every day at the time you chose.

Updating to a new version

When we email a new version, unzip it into the same QuickJobs folder and replace the files. Then open chrome://extensions and click the reload arrow on the QuickJobs card. Your settings stay as they are.

Don't remove QuickJobs and add it again: that deletes the CV and details kept on your computer.

If something goes wrong

  • "Manifest file is missing": you picked the wrong folder. Choose the one with manifest.json inside.
  • QuickJobs disappeared: its folder was moved or deleted. Put it back and click Load unpacked again.
  • No code email: check Spam, wait a minute, then click Send a new code.
  • Chrome warns about developer-mode extensions: choose to keep them. It's expected while QuickJobs is in beta.
Questions

What people ask before they join.

Something else on your mind? Write to quickjobsbeta@gmail.com

Will my current employer find out?

QuickJobs never applies to your current company: it's added to your blocked list from your profile, and you can add others. It doesn't change your profiles or visibility settings on any job site; those stay as you set them.

Is it safe for my Naukri account?

It applies as you, from your own Chrome, at a person's pace and within daily limits you set. It stops at any "verify you're human" check instead of trying to get past it. Job sites set their own rules about automated tools, so we keep limits conservative and you can lower them any time.

Do I need to keep my laptop on?

Chrome needs to be running at the run time you choose. If your laptop was asleep, the run catches up when Chrome opens, and you get an email if a run is missed.

What does it cost?

Weekly plans, priced in rupees, with a cheaper monthly option. Prices are announced before launch. Early users from the waitlist get their first 30 days free.

What does the AI see?

To score a job, QuickJobs sends the job description and your target roles, title, experience, skills, cities and expected pay to its AI provider. Your phone number, current pay, date of birth and gender are never sent. Reading your CV to fill in setup is optional.

Can I approve every job myself instead?

Yes. That's the default. Apply without asking me is a switch you turn on when you're ready, and you can turn it off any day.

What do I need?

Chrome on a Windows, Mac or Linux computer, and accounts on the job sites you want searched. Phones aren't supported yet.
